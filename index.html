Research vision

Engineering compartments that behave more like life.

My research explores how coacervates, vesicles, and nanoparticles can be programmed as biomimetic platforms and therapeutic materials. I combine molecular self-assembly with microfluidic engineering to control where reactions happen, how compartments communicate, and how complex systems emerge — then use robotics and machine learning to accelerate that discovery cycle.
